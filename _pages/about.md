I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Harvard University. My research develops econometric methods for causal questions in settings where the data are complex, high-dimensional, partially informative, or measured with noise. I am especially interested in how ideas from statistics and machine learning can be adapted to improve identification, estimation, and inference for treatment effects and related economic parameters.
